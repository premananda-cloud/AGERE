## Goal for this push

Move the hover/stabilize model from Stage 2 (reached 2026-07-31) to
Stage 3, per `docs/hover-model-plan.md` §4. Stage 3 requires, verbatim:

1. Stage 2 criteria hold across **at least 3 different random seeds**
   (rules out "got lucky once")
2. Mean position error at episode end **< 0.1 m**
3. Recovers from a **mid-episode external disturbance** within a few
   seconds

**Seed clarification settled today:** "different random seeds" +
"rules out got lucky once" means *training* seeds, not eval-sample
seeds — train the policy from scratch 3+ times with different seeds and
confirm each independently-trained policy clears Stage 2 (and ideally
0.1 m). Resampling eval episodes against one existing policy doesn't
address this.

### 2. Baseline re-evaluated with instrumentation
Original Stage 2 run (2026-07-31, logged in `training-log.md`) reported
mean position error 0.088 m with 2/20 tail episodes at 0.24–0.29 m.
Neither the per-episode start condition nor the reason for those tail
episodes was recoverable from that run — `evaluate.py` didn't log start
position, and `gym_wrapper.py`'s `reset()` didn't expose it.

**Fix:** instrumented `reset()` to return `start_position` and
`start_yaw_rad` in its info dict, and `evaluate.py` to capture and log
them, plus added `--seed` (seeds only the first reset; gymnasium's RNG
carries forward deterministically from there, making the whole 20-episode
sequence reproducible for rerun/comparison).

**Result of `python -m src.training.evaluate --seed 42`** (same model as
the 07-31 run, different — but now reproducible — eval sample):

- Mean final position error: **0.099 m** (right at the Stage 3 bar, not
  comfortably under it)
- Crash rate: 0%
- **4/20 tail episodes** this time (>0.2 m threshold), vs. 2/20 in the
  original run: episodes 10 (0.208 m), 13 (0.302 m), 15 (0.228 m),
  18 (0.215 m)

**Notable finding independent of the tail's cause:** the mean flipped
from comfortably-under-0.1 m (0.088 m) to right-at-the-boundary
(0.099 m) just from a different eval sample of the *same* policy. That
means "mean < 0.1 m" isn't yet a stable property of this policy — it's
sensitive to which 20 episodes get drawn. Worth remembering when judging
whether a future retrain "passes" Stage 3's 0.1 m bar on one eval run.

### 3. Tail diagnosis — start-condition correlation
Added jitter-magnitude (distance from target) and yaw-jitter logging,
plus a Pearson correlation between each and final position error.

**Results:**
- Position jitter vs. final error: **+0.27** (ambiguous — inspecting the
  raw pairs directly confirms it's not a real effect: episode 2 has the
  largest jitter in the whole set, 0.407 m, but one of the smallest
  errors, 0.028 m; episode 15 has below-average jitter, 0.221 m, and is a
  tail episode)
- Yaw jitter vs. final error: **+0.07** (no relationship)

**Conclusion:** the tail is not explained by "started further/more
rotated than usual." Ruled out as a start-condition artifact.

### 4. Tail diagnosis — trajectory shape
Added per-step position-error tracking so each tail episode could be
classified as "converged then drifted" vs. "never converged" (best value
reached, and when).

**Result: all 4 tail episodes tagged "never converged."** But the shapes
differ meaningfully within that label:
- **Episode 18** — best value at step 239/240 (last step of the
  episode) — looks like it was still actively closing the gap and simply
  ran out of episode length.
- **Episode 15** — best value at step 42/240, then flat for ~200
  steps — plateaued early and made ~no further progress. More concerning
  shape than the others; not obviously a "just needs more time" case.
- **Episodes 10, 13** — best value mid-to-late episode, each ending
  slightly worse than its own best.

**Conclusion:** genuine convergence-speed/completeness issue, not a
start-condition or late-episode-stability (drift) issue. Two candidate
explanations remain open: undertraining (more timesteps would fix it) vs.
a specific policy blind spot (reward shape issue, per
`hover-model-plan.md` §5's note that smoothness/survival terms could be
competing with the position term).

### 5. Training seed support added (prep for multi-seed step)
`ppo_policy.py`'s `build_ppo()` and `train.py` had no seed parameter at
all — no way to run reproducible/varied training for the 3-seed
requirement. Added `--seed` to `train.py`, passed through to SB3's
`PPO(..., seed=...)`. Saved filename is now seed-aware
(`hover_stabilize_ppo_seed{N}.zip`) so multiple seeded training runs don't
overwrite each other. Omitting `--seed` preserves old behavior exactly
(SB3's own default seeding, filename `hover_stabilize_ppo.zip`).

**Also found:** `device="cpu"` was already set in `ppo_policy.py` —
contradicts `status.md`'s "Not yet done as of this handoff" note. No code
change needed here; `status.md` should be corrected (not done today).
